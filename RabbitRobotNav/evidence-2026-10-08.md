## What the evidence supports

- **Historical replay:** 1,149 raw states and 3,477 semantic observations across archived, separated sessions. Repeated observations are not unique physical objects; there are zero admitted trusted states and zero confirmed metric landmarks. The plotted blue points are camera poses, not chassis poses. Object projections remain unverified; no walls, free space, or cross-session alignment are inferred. [Replay protocol](https://github.com/lijinghai/history-aware-mp3d-vln/tree/9433e164151164ed1801a174255ef73453149b15/docs/evidence/chassis/wheeltec-mecanum/phase1c3-real-robot-semantic-history-v0-2026-10-08).
- **Read-only diagnosis:** all 1,149 historical states have insufficient instruction/history/transition consistency evidence, and no reliable anchor is available. A lack of supported alarms is not a zero-error result. The live attempt stopped at its initialization memory limit before processing any frames. [Diagnostic audit](https://github.com/lijinghai/history-aware-mp3d-vln/tree/9433e164151164ed1801a174255ef73453149b15/docs/evidence/chassis/wheeltec-mecanum/phase1c3-g5-real-history-shadow-2026-10-08).
- **Independent stationary room preflight:** 179 laser scans in 15.004 seconds, approximately 12.04 Hz; 31.72% of beams were valid. Three RGB images were saved. There is no occupancy grid or valid odom-to-map transform, so room coverage is unavailable, not zero. Object detection was not run. These frames do not extend the historical replay denominator. A human-supervised survey remains pending. [Room preflight](https://github.com/lijinghai/history-aware-mp3d-vln/tree/9433e164151164ed1801a174255ef73453149b15/docs/evidence/chassis/wheeltec-mecanum/room-map-v1-2026-10-08).

## Unchanged earlier results

The page retains the controlled MP3D mechanism study (3/4 deviation types detected, 0/34 normal reference points alerted, a 2.44 m pose jump missed), the 110-episode paired CMA comparison (no navigation metric improvement), and the same-chair Trial 1R identity failure (`obj_0000` to `obj_0034`). Their figures and videos are existing research assets, not new October 8 trials. Sparse reference waypoints are not dense executed paths, and a triggered correction is not demonstrated physical recovery. The retained chair failure and the G5 diagnostic boundary use different time points and must not be conflated.
