## What changed since 8 October

### Manual room survey: captured, not accepted

The earlier stationary preflight did not produce an occupancy grid. The subsequent **human-controlled** survey recorded approximately 229 seconds, 2,747 laser scans, 4,580 raw odometry messages, 2,281 fused odometry messages, and 225 RGB keyframes (224 with camera intrinsics). A 427 × 584 occupancy grid at 0.05 m resolution was obtained. This replaces the outdated statement that the survey is still pending. [Capture protocol](https://github.com/lijinghai/history-aware-mp3d-vln/tree/18957ee8ec62470ac2d57dbe72c6c7ab0d437dc9/docs/evidence/chassis/wheeltec-mecanum/room-map-v1-live-survey-2026-10-09).

The status remains `ROOM_GEOMETRY_CAPTURED_PENDING_REVIEW` / `ROOM_MAP_V1=PENDING_REVIEW`. Radial streaks, sparse wall structure and inconsistent return estimates require review. The source image's filename `room_semantic_map.png` does **not** establish semantic mapping: the detector was `NOT_RUN`, no physical instance was verified, and trusted metric landmarks remain zero. Its “measured odom path” label refers to integrated odometry estimates, not independently measured physical distance. The survey is separate from the 1,149-state archived history replay.

### Heading investigation: failed candidate, supported source attribution

The geometry audit found that fused yaw and angular velocity remained zero while raw odometry recorded turns. Map artifacts do not have an independently established single cause. [Geometry audit](https://github.com/lijinghai/history-aware-mp3d-vln/tree/18957ee8ec62470ac2d57dbe72c6c7ab0d437dc9/docs/evidence/chassis/wheeltec-mecanum/room-map-v1-geometry-audit-2026-10-09).

The subsequent A/B used the actual ROS 2 `robot_localization` EKF in network-isolated runs with the same 4,580 recorded odometry messages. The only research variable was `odom0_config[11]`, enabling angular-z velocity fusion. Candidate yaw changed by −0.077352249 rad, compared with −7.121530533 rad in recorded raw odometry and zero in the isolated baseline. Rounded values on the page are −0.077 and −7.122 rad. The status is `EKF_ISOLATED_AB_CANDIDATE_FAILED`, not “heading fixed.” These fresh-start runs are not a full recreation of historical filter initialization; the survey bag lacks synchronized IMU data. Raw odometry is not orientation ground truth. No candidate was deployed to the robot. [A/B protocol](https://github.com/lijinghai/history-aware-mp3d-vln/tree/18957ee8ec62470ac2d57dbe72c6c7ab0d437dc9/docs/evidence/chassis/wheeltec-mecanum/ekf-isolated-heading-ab-v1-2026-10-09).

The read-only covariance audit matched the driver source, executable constants, and all 4,580 recorded messages: when any of the three velocity components is nonzero, `twist.covariance[35]` is 1,000 (rad/s)²; when all are exactly zero it is 1e−9. This supports strong down-weighting of angular velocity in the tested candidate, not a calibrated replacement value or a validated fix. Pose-yaw variance and angular-velocity variance have different units and are not interchangeable. The source snapshot was inspected at `Publish_Odom` and the four covariance arrays. [Covariance audit and source](https://github.com/lijinghai/history-aware-mp3d-vln/tree/18957ee8ec62470ac2d57dbe72c6c7ab0d437dc9/docs/evidence/chassis/wheeltec-mecanum/wheeltec-angular-covariance-audit-2026-10-09).
